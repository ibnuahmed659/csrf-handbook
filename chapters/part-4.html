QAYBTA IV

QAYBTA IV DEFENSES

  • 10 qaybood
  • 12 tusaale
  • 2 jadwal

Waxaa jira khalad weyn oo bug hunters badan sameeyaan. Waxay u arkaan defenses-ka sida code. Waxay yiraahdaan: "Haddii code-ku jiro, defense-ku wuu shaqeynayaa." Laakiin taasi waa been.

Difaacu waa fikrad. Waa falsafad. Waa assumption. . Code-ku wuxuu kaliya implement gareeyaa fikradda. Haddii fikraddu khaldan tahay, code-ku wuu khaldan yahay. Haddii fikraddu jaban tahay, code-ku wuu jaban yahay.

Defense kastaa wuxuu ku salaysan yahay male-awaal. Wuxuu yiraahdaa: "Attacker-ku ma sameyn karo waxan." Wuxuu yiraahdaa: "Browser-ku wuxuu u dhaqmaa sidaan." Wuxuu yiraahdaa: "User-ku wuxuu sameeyaa sidaas." Haddii male-awaalku khaldan yahay, defense-ku wuu khaldamaa.

CSRF Token

CSRF token waa qoraal sir ah oo lagu daro form-ka. Ujeeddadiisu waa:

"Haddii attacker-ku aanu garanayn token-ka, ma diri karo request sax ah."

Token-ku waa caqabad. Waa obstacle. Waa secret. Wuxuu ku salaysan yahay male-awaalka:

  1. Attacker-ku ma garanayo token-ka.
  2. Attacker-ku ma akhrin karo token-ka (sababtoo ah SOP).
  3. Attacker-ku ma saadaalin karo token-ka (sababtoo ah waa random).

Haddii mid ka mid ah male-awaalkan khaldan yahay, token-ku waa la dhaafi karaa

Noocyada CSRF Token

1. Synchronizer Token PatternToken-ka waxaa lagu kaydiyaa session-ka. Marka request-ku yimaado, server-ku wuxuu barbardhigaa token-ka body-ga iyo session-ka.

Token-ku waa session-bound. Waa user-specific. Waa stateful.

Haddii token-ku aanu session-ka ku xirneyn , waa la jabsan karaa.

2. Double Submit CookieToken-ka waxaa lagu diraa cookie iyo body. Server-ku wuxuu is barbardhigaa labadaba.

Haddii attacker-ku uu cookie-ga ku soo dhex geli karo browser-ka victim (CRLF injection), waa la jabi karaa.

3. Encrypted Token PatternToken-ka waa encrypted. Server-ku wuxuu decrypt-gareeyaa si uu u xaqiijiyo.

4. HMAC Token PatternToken-ka waa HMAC oo lagu sameeyay session-ka.

4.2.3 Sida Loo Jebiyo CSRF Token

Token-ku wuxuu leeyahay shuruudo oo waa in uu buuxiyaa. Haddii mid ka mid ah aanu buuxin, waa la jabin karaa.

ShuruudHaddii aan la buuxinBypass

Session-boundToken aan session ku xirneynToken user A → user B
Single-useToken la isticmaali karo marar badanReuse

Token-ku waa defense. Waa caqabad. Waa secret. Laakiin defense kastaa waa la jabsan karaa haddii male-awaalku khaldan yahay.

SameSite: Falsafadda Xaddidaadda

SameSite waa cookie attribute oo xaddida cross-site behavior. Wuxuu leeyahay saddex qiime:

1. SameSite=Strict

Strict waa isolation. Waa maximal security. Waa minimal convenience.

  1. User-ku wuxuu ku qasban yahay inuu same-site u baahan yahay.
  2. Cross-site requests waa khatar.
  3. Cookie-ga waa qarsoodi.
  • Client-side redirect .
  • Sibling domain .
  • XSS on same-site.

2. SameSite=Lax

  1. GET waa safe — ma beddelo state.
  2. Top-level navigation waa user-intended.
  3. POST waa khatar — wuu beddelaa state.
  4. GET + method override (lab-kii hore).
  5. Cookie refresh (lab-kii hore).

3. SameSite=None

  1. Cross-site requests waa lagama maarmaan.
  2. User-ku wuxuu ku qasban yahay inuu cross-site u baahan yahay.
  3. Security waa secondary.
  4. CSRF waa mid fudud.

4.3.2 Sida Loo Jebiyo SameSite

SameSiteBypass
StrictClient-side redirect, sibling domain, XSS
LaxMethod override, cookie refresh, top-level GET
NoneCSRF waa mid fudud

SameSite waa defense. Waa rule. Laakiin rule kastaa wuu leeyahay exception. Attacker-ku wuxuu raadiyaa exception-ka.
