QAYBTA I

QAYBTA I — FALSAFADDA CSRF

  • 5 qaybood
  • 1 tusaale

Aan ku bilowno su'aal fudud oo aan la weydiin:

Markaad bangigaaga gasho, browser-kaagu wuxuu hayaa cookie yar oo la yiraahdo session. Cookie-gaas wuxuu yiraahdaa: "Kani waa Axmed." Server-ku wuu aqbalayaa. Waxba lama weydiiyo. Ma jiro sawir, ma jiro cod, ma jiro wax. Kaliya cookie.

Hadda, aan is weydiino su'aal qoto dheer:

Cookie-gaas ma caddaynayaa in "adiga" aad tahay adiga? Mise wuxuu caddaynayaa kaliya in browser-kaagu hayo cookie-gaas?

Halkan ayey CSRF ku dhalatay..

HTTP iyo Statelessness:

Si aad u fahanto CSRF, waa in aad fahanto HTTP. HTTP waa stateless protocol. Erayga "stateless" macneheedu waa: HTTP ma xasuusto wax. Request kastaa waa qof cusub oo aan la aqoon. Request kastaa waa "stranger" server-ka.

Tusaale:

  • Request 1: GET /login — server-ku wuu ku garanayaa.
  • Request 2: POST /login — server-ku wuu ku garanayaa.
  • Request 3: GET /my-account — server-ku ma garanayo ciddaad tahay. Waa qof cusub.

HTTP laftiisu wuxuu leeyahay amnesia. Wuxuu illoobaa request kasta ka dib.

Marka waxaa la alifay cookies.

Cookie waa badge yar oo browser-kaagu hayo. Markaad login sameyso, server-ku wuxuu ku siiyaa badge: session=ABC123. Browser-kaagu wuxuu badge-kaas u diraa request kasta oo u socda domain-kaas.

Dhibaatada

Halkan ayey wax walba ka xumaadaan. Browser-kaagu waa confused deputy.

Erayga "confused deputy" wuxuu la micno yahay: qof awood leh oo aan garanayn cidda uu u shaqeynayo.

Browser-kaagu wuxuu hayaa cookies badan oo domains badan. Wuxuu hayaa:

  • session cookie-ga bangigaaga.
  • session cookie-ga email-kaaga.
  • session cookie-ga shirkaddaada.
  • iwm.

Marka request uu yimaado, browser-kaagu wuxuu diraa cookies-ka domain-ka la codsaday. Ma weydiiyo: "Yaa request-kan bilaabay?" Wuxuu weydiiyaa kaliya: "Domain-kee ayaa la codsaday?"

Browser-kaagu wuxuu u shaqeeyaa domain-ka, uma shaqeeyo user-ka. Wuxuu yahay confused deputy — wuxuu hayaa awood (cookies), laakiin ma garanayo cidda uu u shaqeynayo.

Marka attacker uu yiraahdo browser-ka victim: "Dir request u socda bangiga", browser-ku wuxuu diraa cookies-ka bangiga. Server-ku wuxuu u malaynayaa in victim isagu request-ka diray. Laakiin attacker ayaa diray.

CSRF waa exploit-ka confused deputy.

Aan si qoto dheer u dhuuxno. Maxay tahay trust?

Trust waa saadaalin marka la joogo web pen testing. Markaad aaminsan tahay qof, waxaad saadaalinaysaa inuu sidaas sameyn doono. Server-ku wuxuu aaminsan yahay cookie-ga. Wuxuu saadaalinayaa: "Haddii cookie-ga la soo diro, user-kaas ayaa request-ka diray."

Laakiin trust Waa assumption. CSRF wuxuu ka faa'iidaystaa male-awaalkaas.

Server-ku wuxuu isku darayaa identity iyo authorization . Wuxuu u malaynayaa: "Haddii aan ku aqoonsado, fasax ayaad haystaa."

CSRF wuxuu ka faa'iidaystaa khaladkan falsafadeed.

Mental Model

Hadda aan isku dhisno mental model oo aad isticmaali doonto lab kasta.

Model-ka waa sidan:

muxuu CSRF u jiraa?

CSRF wuxuu u jiraa saddex waxyaabood oo aan laga baxsan karin:

1. HTTP waa stateless. Waa in la isticmaalaa cookies si loo hayo state. Tani waa aasaas. Lama beddeli karo.

2. Browser-ku wuxuu diraa cookies si toos ah. Waa in browser-ku u shaqeeyaa sidaas si loo fududeeyo isticmaalka. Tani waa convenience. Waa trade-off.

3. Web-ku wuxuu leeyahay cross-origin requests. Waa in website-ku u ogolaadaa requests kale (images, scripts, iwm). Tani waa interoperability. Waa aasaas.

Saddexdan isku darkooda waxay abuuraan CSRF. Lama tirtiri karo midkoodna..
